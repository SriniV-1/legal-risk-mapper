PRIVACY POLICY — MedTrack Application
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We collect personally identifiable information (PII) including your name, email, location
data, biometric data from our health sensors, and browsing behavior across our platform.
We use cookies and tracking technologies to monitor your activity.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
Your personal data and health information may be shared with third-party research partners
and pharmaceutical companies. We may sell anonymized user data. We retain all data
indefinitely unless required otherwise by applicable law.

3. HIPAA NOTICE
Our platform processes protected health information. We must comply with HIPAA requirements
for covered entities. In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within
60 days as required by applicable regulations.

4. CCPA RIGHTS
California residents may opt-out of the sale of their personal information pursuant to
the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Contact privacy@medtrack.example to exercise
your rights.

5. GDPR (EU USERS)
For EU users, we process personal data under legitimate interest as deemed appropriate by
our legal team. Data may be transferred to the United States without adequate safeguards.
Users may request deletion, subject to retention requirements under applicable law.

6. SECURITY
We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. However, no system is completely
secure, and we disclaim any liability for unauthorized access or data breaches.

7. POLICY CHANGES
We may update this policy from time to time at our sole discretion without prior notice.
Your continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
